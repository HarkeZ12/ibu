IT 2003 Ethical and Legal Issues of IT - Week 06: Ethical and Legal Aspects of Data Science
===========================================================================================

How to open it
1. Extract the ZIP first (right-click > Extract All). Do not open the file
   directly from inside the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted "week06" folder and double-click "reading-06.html".
   It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox...).
3. Keep the "images" folder next to "reading-06.html", otherwise the
   figures will not show.

What is inside
- Reading tab: the full study guide for Chapter 5 (19 sections, 5 figures).
- Quiz tab: 177 questions with 5 options each. Before you start, choose:
  - Answers: "One correct answer" (select one) or "Several correct answers"
    (select two or more; always 2 to 4 are correct, and a question counts
    only if you pick every correct option and no wrong one).
  Single mode includes all 177 questions; multi mode includes the 176
  questions with at least two correct statements.
  - Difficulty: Easy (34, obvious common-sense answers), Medium (108,
    normal exam-style questions) and/or Hard (35, traps and multi-step
    scenarios).
  - Practice mode (feedback after every question) or Exam mode (results at
    the end).
  Options are drawn from a larger pool on every run, so the same question
  can show different answers next time. Mistake review keeps the original
  options (in a new order) when available for the selected answer type.

No installation is needed. Without internet everything still works; only the
fonts fall back to the standard system fonts.

Online version: https://harkez12.github.io/ibu/

Study features
- Saved mistakes persist in this browser across sessions. Select topics and
  levels and choose Review saved mistakes to practice them. A later correct
  answer removes that question.
- Battery saver in the top bar removes backdrop blur and grain and reduces
  visual effects. Its setting is remembered in this browser.
- Saving needs browser storage; private browsing or clearing browser data can
  remove saved sessions, mistakes and settings.

Source: G. O'Regan, "Ethical and Legal Aspects of Computing" (Springer, 2024),
Chapter 5 "Ethical and Legal Aspects of Data Science". IT 2003, International
Burch University, 2026/2027.

Review revision: all 35 Hard questions are scenario/distinction questions with
close wrong options, and each wrong option has its own explanation. All quiz
questions follow the chapter as written. The Reading introduction has a box
with clarifications and official links where the chapter simplifies the law;
those notes are extra context and are not asked in the quiz.
