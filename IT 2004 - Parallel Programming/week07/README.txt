IT 2004 Parallel Programming - Week 07: OpenMP that Performs
============================================================

How to open it
1. Extract the ZIP first (right-click > Extract All). Do not open the file
   directly from inside the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted "week07" folder and double-click "reading-07.html".
   It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox...).
3. Keep the "images" folder next to "reading-07.html", otherwise the
   figures will not show.

What is inside
- Reading tab: the full study guide for Chapter 7 (13 sections, 16 figures).
- Study notes tab: the most important points of every section on one card:
  the big idea, what to remember, the usual exam trap and flashcards to test
  yourself. Also "Numbers to remember", a pragma cheat sheet and the top exam
  traps. Every reading section links to its card and back.
- Quiz tab: 175 questions, five options and exactly one correct answer each,
  in three levels: Easy (common sense), Medium (normal exam questions) and
  Hard (traps and subtle details). Choose Practice mode (feedback after every
  question) or Exam mode (results at the end).

No installation is needed. Without internet everything still works; only the
fonts fall back to the standard system fonts.

Online version: https://harkez12.github.io/ibu/

Study features
- "Mark as learned" on each study-notes card is remembered in this browser.
- Saved mistakes persist in this browser across sessions. Select topics and
  choose Review saved mistakes to practice them. A later correct answer removes
  that question. Changes to a question invalidate its old mistake entry.
- Battery saver in the top bar removes backdrop blur and grain and reduces
  visual effects. Its setting is remembered in this browser.
- Saving needs browser storage; private browsing or clearing browser data can
  remove saved sessions, mistakes and settings.
