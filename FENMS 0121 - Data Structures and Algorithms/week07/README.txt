FENMS 0121 Data Structures and Algorithms - Week 07: Quick Sort
================================================================

How to open it
1. Extract the ZIP first (right-click > Extract All). Do not open the file
   directly from inside the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted "week07" folder and double-click "reading-07.html".
   It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox...).
3. Keep the "images" folder next to "reading-07.html", otherwise the
   figures will not show.

What is inside
- Reading tab: the full study guide for Reading 07 "Quick Sort"
  (13 sections, 3 figures). Boxes marked "From the lecture" add what the
  lecture slides "07 · Quick Sort" say about the same topic.
- Three live demos in the reading: type your own array and step through
  Sedgewick's two-way partitioning, list every call of sort(low, high),
  or step through dual-pivot partitioning.
- Study notes tab: the most important points of every section on one card:
  the big idea, the usual exam trap and flashcards. Also "Names and
  numbers", "Tracing a partition by hand", "Only on the lecture slides" and
  the top exam traps.
- Quiz tab: 164 questions from the reading material, including trace
  questions (array after a partition, returned index, swaps, pivot order,
  dual-pivot, Knuth shuffle). Trace questions have a link that opens their
  array in the live demo.
- Slides quiz tab: 89 questions only from the lecture slides (the code and
  its line numbers, the call table, the run-time and comparison tables,
  and the slides' examples).
  Every question has five options and exactly one correct answer. Choose
  the levels Easy (common sense), Medium (normal exam questions) and Hard
  (traps and traces), and Practice mode (feedback after every question) or
  Exam mode (results at the end).

No installation is needed. Without internet everything still works; only the
fonts fall back to the standard system fonts.

Online version: https://harkez12.github.io/ibu/

Study features
- "Mark as learned" on each study-notes card is remembered in this browser.
- Saved mistakes persist in this browser across sessions, separately for the
  reading quiz and the slides quiz. Select topics and choose Review saved
  mistakes to practice them. A later correct answer removes that question.
  Changes to a question invalidate its old mistake entry.
- Battery saver in the top bar removes backdrop blur and grain and reduces
  visual effects. Its setting is remembered in this browser.
- Saving needs browser storage; private browsing or clearing browser data can
  remove saved sessions, mistakes and settings.

Source: FENMS 0121 Data Structures and Algorithms, Reading 07 "Quick Sort"
(study notes) and the lecture slides "07 · Quick Sort", International Burch
University, 2026/2027.
