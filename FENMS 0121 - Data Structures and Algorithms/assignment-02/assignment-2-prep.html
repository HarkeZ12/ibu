Practical and coding questions from weeks 3, 4 and 5, the way the assignment asks them. Code is compiled by a real C++ compiler running in your browser; traces and drawings are checked step by step. Your answers stay in this browser.

In the assignment you get lab code with a missing method or missing lines, and you fill them in. Pick a task in the list. Lab tasks have two modes:
  • Whole method: the body is empty, write all of it.
  • Missing lines: one to three lines are removed; New gaps removes different ones.
  • The Practice groups are the LMS practice questions from weeks 3–5.

Exceptions: this in-browser compiler cannot catch exceptions. throw works: it ends the test and shows as [throws std::out_of_range], which is how the tests check it. A try/catch block compiles but its catch part never runs, so do not rely on it here (it works normally in CLion and on the LMS).
